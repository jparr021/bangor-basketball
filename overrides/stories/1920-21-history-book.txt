# Basket Ball 1920–21
The basket ball season for 1920–21 was started the second week in November. As we had no gymnasium the school board rented the Concordia hall once more for the basket ball team. The boys did not have a coach to start with, but every one was determined to put forth as good a basket ball team as possible. There were a few men left from last year's team and a number from last year's second team that furnished some good material.
> The line-up: Left Forward, Stintzi. Right Forward, Wolters. Center, Thomas (captain). Right Guard, Bonsack. Left Guard, Meinking.
## Bangor 11, Onalaska Aggies 16
After two weeks of practice the team met the Onalaska Aggies on the home floor for the first game of the season. The team fought a hard battle but the end of the game found them on the short end of the score. This game did not count against our high school record but it showed that the team needed a coach.
## Bangor 13, Westby 3
December 13 the team met the Westby High School team on the home floor. This time however they were determined to win and at the end they were victorious. At this time the school board secured Coach Ettinger from the La Crosse Normal to come up twice a week.
## Bangor 4, West Salem 7
Friday December 17 the team journeyed to West Salem for a battle with the Salemites. The game dragged a little in the first half due to the out of bounds rule, nevertheless neither team scored, the first half ending 0-0. In the second half both teams came out with their minds set to score. Except for a number of times-out the game progressed rapidly. At 20 seconds to play the score was 4-3 in favor of Bangor when one of our players was laid out. This was Bangor's third time out and the referee called a foul on the Captain for it. Whether he did not know the rules or made a mistake is not known but no foul should have been called. Salem made the free throw which made the score 4-4 at the end of the game. An extra five minutes were played in which Salem made a free throw and a field basket making the score 7-4 in favor of Salem. This was the first time that West Salem ever defeated Bangor in anything along the line of athletics and the title to this game is doubtful.
## Bangor 13, Alumni 17
Christmas vacation interferred with practice although the team met the Alumni on December 30. The Alumni was very strong this year as some of the players played on college fives and were just home on their vacation. This was another defeat for the team although they put up a good fight against the strong Alumni team, the score being 17-13 in favor of the Alumni.

## Bangor 27, West Salem 14
… Salem to one field basket and two free throws while they (Bangor) ran up the score several points, the game ending 27-14. This settled, to some extent, our previous defeat.
## Bangor 12, Onalaska Aggies 38
February 28 we played the return game with the Aggies. The Aggies were much stronger now, having taken on some new men. We took a second defeat from them by a score of 38-12. This game was rather a farce because no fouls were called although both sides fouled considerably, it being not uncommon to see a player run half the length of the floor with the ball.
## Bangor 11, Galesville 7
February 25 the team traveled to Galesville for the return game. Galesville was determined to win but we would not allow it. We secured the lead at the beginning and kept it all the way through. The first half ended 6-5 in our favor but in the second half we came out stronger and defeated them 11-7. This was another game in which Bangor showed a lot of pep.
Bangor was one of the eight teams chosen for the district tournament held at La Crosse Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, March 10, 11, and 12.
## Bangor 12, Prairie du Chien 22
We drew Prairie du Chien, the second strongest team at the tournament, for the second game on the afternoon of the first day. We were at the disadvantage of having to play on a much larger floor than we were accustomed to. Nevertheless the boys played a hard and fast game the first half of which ended 9-7 with Bangor on the short end. The boys returned for the second half just as strong and full of pep but, owing to Prairie's marvelous luck on long shots they were unable to keep up with them. The game ended 22-12 in favor of Prairie du Chien.
## Bangor 9, Hillsboro 17
Our defeat by Prairie put us up against Hillsboro, who had been defeated by Tomah, for the next afternoon. We played hard but were again defeated by a score of 17-9, one free throw being made by Thomas after the time was up. Although we were beaten in two games and thereby ruled out of the tournament our team received only the highest words of praise from outsiders who saw them play.
Our last game was to be played with Onalaska at Onalaska on March 18. The Onalaska team had broken up so they canceled the game, or, in other words, forfeited it to Bangor.
# The players
## Royal Stintzi, “Skinny,” 1917–21
Basket ball is one of his hobbies. As forward he showed good head work. He fooled his opponents by appearing “all in.” When they least expected it he would cage a basket. Boydie enjoyed himself as referee of a girl's game.
## John Thomas, “Jack,” 1917–21
Last year Jack successfully captained the team. He played center this year. He had a dead eye from the foul lines. He shot all free throws and tried to do this scientifically.
## Lyle Bonsack, “Polly,” 1919–21
Polly played guard. He understands basket ball. To him next year will go the honors of the captaincy. Success to you Lyle.
## Albert Aichele, “Al,” 1919–21
“Al” played running guard. He, although not as experienced as the other players, showed remarkable head work and speed in the game. The opponents forwards were never able to pass the ball near him.
## William Wolters, “Spotty,” 1918–21
William acted as “sub” his freshman year. This year he played forward and held down his place well. Later he was shifted to center. We are glad he has two more years to play. Come on there Bill!
# Girls' Basket Ball
The girls' basket ball team was organized about the middle of February under the supervision of Miss Page. This movement was not taken before because of the absence of a coach. Owing to the fact that the basket ball season was nearly over they were unable to schedule many games with outside teams.
The Girls' Class games were played on March 4; in which the Seniors and the Freshmen were defeated by the Juniors and Sophomores, by the score thirteen to eight.
Two games were scheduled with Camp Douglas; the first on March 29, to be played at Camp Douglas, and the second on April 1, to be played at Bangor. The girls although they put up good games were defeated in both games. The score of the first game was fourteen to four; and that of the second was four to two. Nevertheless they have not lost hope for the future. There is good material among the girls of High School for a basket ball team next year.
> The line-up: Jumping Center (Captain), Charlotte Mengelt. Running Center (Manager), Ruth Fletcher. Right Forward, Hilda Klein. Left Forward, Irma Holberg. Right Guard, Margaret Westcott. Left Guard, Naomi Jones. Substitutes, Sarah Klein and Anna Williams.
# Hurrah for the New “Gym”
At last the hopes of the school children and many towns-people will come true. It was decided at a special school meeting held April 12th, by a vote of one hundred and one against seventy-eight that Bangor, would have a gymnasium added to its new school house. For a long time we have wished for a “gym” because it will not only be beneficial for the physical training course but also as a place in which the community may hold meetings and have social affairs. The addition of a gymnasium to our school will make it modern in every way.
