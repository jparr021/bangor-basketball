What helps

  • Game programs. Rosters with numbers, heights and grades, plus season results and records printed inside.
  • Yearbook sports pages. Season scores, team photos, and sometimes team and player stats.
  • Newspaper clippings. Box scores, quarter scores and game stories from the La Crosse Tribune, the county papers and the Bangor paper. Even a single game helps.
  • Scorebooks and stat sheets. A coach's or manager's book can fill in a whole season of box scores.
  • Photos and film. Team pictures, game photos, and VHS or DVD recordings of games.
  • Corrections. A wrong score, a misspelled name or a missing class year.

A phone photo of each page is fine. Originals aren't needed, and nothing has to be mailed.
